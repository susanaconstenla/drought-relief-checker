What we need

Records of drought relief disbursed in each of the 125 listed wards, for every month from January 2024 to the present, and by implementing organization where possible. The ward-month structure is essential: figures added up to county level or to a full year cannot be used in the analysis.

Required vs optional
County, Ward, Month, Type of aid, Amount, Currency / unit and Households served are needed for every row. Distributing agency, Form of payment and Notes are welcome whenever the information is on hand, but a row is still usable without them.
One row per aid type and organization
If a ward received two kinds of assistance in the same month (for example a cash transfer and a food ration), enter two separate rows. If the same type of aid was distributed by different organizations, enter one row for each. If that is not possible, enter the total and explain in Notes.
Months with no aid
If no aid was given in a month, record it with amount 0 (Type of aid: “No disbursement this month”). This tells a month with no aid apart from a missing record.
Amounts
Enter the total disbursed in that ward-month as a plain number, with no currency symbol or thousands separator, and give the currency in the next column: KES or USD. For in-kind aid, enter not applicable as the amount and choose Not applicable (in kind) as the unit; if an estimated monetary value is available, add it in Notes. The portal removes separators like 3,000.00 for you.
Households served
The number of distinct households that received the aid in that ward-month. If only counts of individuals exist, enter those and say so in Notes.
Form of payment
The schedule of the transfer. For example, KES 3,000 per month for two months is “Monthly instalments”, with the per-instalment detail in Notes.
Ward list
Choose the county first: the Ward column then suggests only that county's wards. The 125 wards in scope are in 22 ASAL counties. Rows for other wards, or a ward entered under the wrong county, are flagged so you can check them.
Partial data is welcome
If some wards or months are not feasible for your team, submit what you have. Get in touch and we can try to narrow the list.
